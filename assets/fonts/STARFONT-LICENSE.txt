StarFont Sans public-domain notice

The bundled starfont-sans.ttf is the original StarFont Sans TrueType font designed by Anthony I. P. Owen. It matches STRFNSAN.TTF from the CTAN starfont 1.2 package.

Source: https://ctan.org/pkg/starfont
SHA-256: dde004a71fc268451ebb4d7c4ace3c3561b59a3ffb1109b81dfffce84491b29c

As of September 29, 2010, the starfont package was released to the public domain by its authors, Matthew Skala and Anthony I. P. Owen.

Anthony Owen confirmed that he placed the StarFont Serif and StarFont Sans fonts in the public domain, with no licensing restrictions, and permitted distribution as-is without implied warranty. Matthew Skala also released his packaging contributions to the public domain.
